A field guide to wrapped cloth, Mexico & Tamil Nadu

One length of cloth, five ways to wear it.

A rebozo and a saree are both, at heart, a single uncut length of woven cloth — no seams, no darts — shaped entirely by how it's folded, tucked, and knotted. Four regional Mexican rebozos, and one Tamil Nadu saree drape, step through the same idea in two textile traditions.
